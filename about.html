About Me

AI/ML Engineer & Data Scientist passionate about building intelligent systems

Hello, I'm Ali Baygi

I'm an AI/ML Engineer and Data Scientist based in Berlin, Germany, specializing in building intelligent real-time data systems and advanced LLM applications. With expertise spanning across artificial intelligence, machine learning, and data engineering, I create solutions that bridge the gap between cutting-edge AI research and practical, scalable production systems.

My work focuses on leveraging modern AI technologies including Large Language Models (LLMs), Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) systems, and real-time data processing to solve complex business problems. I have extensive experience with Azure cloud services, including Azure Databricks, Azure EventHub, and building end-to-end data pipelines for both batch and real-time processing.

I'm passionate about staying at the forefront of AI innovation, constantly exploring new frameworks like LangChain, experimenting with state-of-the-art models, and implementing MLOps best practices to ensure robust, production-ready systems.
